Privacy Policy for DriveAssist

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 | Last Revised: October 18, 2026

DriveAssist is developed and operated by Salina Labs ("Salina Labs", "we", "us", or "our"). We build automotive software under a strict privacy by design architecture. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is processed, encrypted, stored, and protected when you use the DriveAssist mobile application ("Application", "Service").

Core Privacy Guarantee: Zero-Knowledge & Zero-Cloud Speech DriveAssist is built so that you own your data. Private contacts, custom wake words, and destination shortcuts are protected using hardware-backed AES-256-GCM encryption on your device. Speech recognition is executed 100% on-device without cloud audio streaming. Salina Labs does not possess the keys to decrypt your vault or inspect your driving activity.

1. Scope of Android Permissions & Data Processing

DriveAssist requests specific Android operating system permissions strictly to provide hands-free driving utilities, in-cockpit navigation, and emergency assistance. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information to third parties.

Android Permission Functional Purpose Data Handling & Storage Scope
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Calculates real-time vehicle speed, compass bearing, odometer distance, weather telemetry, and automated parking spot coordinates. Processed strictly in local volatile RAM and stored in local database tables. Location data is never transmitted to Salina Labs servers or external location aggregators.
RECORD_AUDIO & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE Powers hands-free wake word detection and speech recognition while driving mode is active. 100% on-device speech processing via Android's local recognizer (EXTRA_PREFER_OFFLINE = true). Audio buffers are processed in 80ms chunks in temporary RAM and immediately overwritten. No audio recordings are saved to storage or transmitted over networks.
READ_CONTACTS Resolves contact names and telephone numbers for the Quick Call Hub and VIP Speed Dials. Processed strictly on-device to match voice calling requests and render speed dial cards. Your device address book is never synced to external servers or copied to cloud databases.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) Delivers high-priority driving safety alerts, Emergency SOS status updates, and foreground trip recording controls. Local system notifications only. No telemetry or messaging payloads are transmitted externally.
CALL_PHONE & SEND_SMS Executes direct speed-dial calls and background Emergency SOS distress dispatches upon driver request. Triggered strictly upon user tap or verified emergency voice alias (e.g., "Help", "Mayday"). SMS messages with live GPS coordinates are sent directly via Android SmsManager to pre-designated emergency contacts.
BLUETOOTH & BLUETOOTH_CONNECT Detects pairing and disconnection with vehicle infotainment systems or Bluetooth car stereos. Used strictly to trigger automated driving mode and record the vehicle's parked GPS coordinates upon Bluetooth disconnection.

2. Advertising Identifiers & Commercial Trackers

DriveAssist is completely ad-free. Our software build configurations explicitly remove and block advertising identifier permissions at the manifest compilation level:

  • No Google Advertising ID: The permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID is removed using manifest strip rules (tools:node="remove").
  • No AdServices Tracking: The permission android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID is explicitly stripped.
  • Zero Commercial Telemetry: DriveAssist contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or data-broker profiling libraries.

3. Offline Voice Architecture & Speech Privacy

We enforce a strict offline acoustic pipeline within the application:

  • LocalSttEngine: Speech recognition is initialized with explicit flags enforcing on-device processing. Spoken audio buffers are streamed through the AudioStreamMultiplexer in volatile RAM and immediately discarded.
  • Non-Intrusive Voice Activity Detection (VAD): Real-time acoustic energy filtering isolates spoken commands without continuously muting or ducking cabin media playback.
  • Isolated Model Directory: Acoustic intent models and grammars reside exclusively in context.noBackupFilesDir, preventing unencrypted duplication to Google Drive or Android cloud backup mirrors.
  • Immediate Audio Cutoff: An Android ACTION_AUDIO_BECOMING_NOISY broadcast receiver terminates Text-to-Speech playback immediately if vehicle Bluetooth or auxiliary cables disconnect, preventing cabin echoes.

4. Zero-Knowledge Cryptographic Vault Architecture

DriveAssist provides a built-in Security Vault for sensitive driver data (emergency contacts, custom voice aliases, and private destinations):

  • Hardware-Backed AES-256-GCM: Cryptographic keys are derived on-device and stored within the hardware secure element (Android Keystore, StrongBox, or Trusted Execution Environment).
  • BIP-39 Standard Recovery Seed: Initial vault setup generates an industry-standard 12-word cryptographic recovery phrase. This seed phrase is the exclusive mathematical key capable of restoring an encrypted vault.
  • Zero-Knowledge Principle: Salina Labs servers and customer support staff do not receive, store, or log your master key or recovery seed phrase. If you lose your master credentials, Salina Labs cannot decrypt or restore your data.

5. Optional Cloud Synchronization

Drivers may optionally enable cloud backup to synchronize preferences across personal devices:

  • Client-Side Encryption Before Transit: All vault records, custom wake words, and settings are encrypted on your physical device using AES-256 before transmission over secure TLS 1.3 channels.
  • Encrypted Payload Storage: Payloads stored in Google Firebase Firestore consist exclusively of encrypted ciphertexts. Salina Labs has no technical mechanism to read or analyze synchronized records.
  • Delta Synchronization: Synchronization operations transfer minimal differential payloads to preserve mobile cellular bandwidth and battery life.

7. Data Retention, Portability & Deletion Rights

You maintain absolute ownership and control over your personal data under global data protection frameworks, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Recently Deleted Retention (90 Days): Accidentally deleted contacts are moved to an isolated local retention bin for 90 days, after which they are permanently shredded from device memory.
  • In-App Cloud Purge: You can purge all remote cloud backups instantly by navigating to Control Center > Data Control > Purge Cloud Backups.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: You can delete your account directly inside the application under System Info > Danger Zone > Permanently Delete Account. This immediately purges your cloud database records and invalidates your authentication credentials.
  • Web-Based Deletion Request: If you cannot access your mobile device, submit an account deletion request by emailing support@salinalabs.dev from your registered email address. All user records will be permanently purged within 30 days.
