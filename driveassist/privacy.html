Privacy Policy for DriveAssist

Effective Date: October 4, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

DriveAssist ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Salina Labs, provides an automotive companion application designed for hands-free utility and cockpit organization. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access, how that data is stored and encrypted, and how we protect your privacy when you use the DriveAssist mobile application ("App").

Security Architecture: DriveAssist uses on-device hardware-backed AES-256 encryption. Your private contacts, custom voice aliases, parking coordinates, and synced configuration toggles are encrypted directly on your device before any optional cloud backup transmission.

1. Device Permissions and Data Accessed

DriveAssist requires specific Android runtime permissions to deliver hands-free driving features:

  • Location Data (Precise and Coarse GPS): Used to render in-app map tiles, display routing, and automatically record your vehicle's parked location when Bluetooth disconnects. Location data is stored locally and is never sold or shared with data brokers.
  • Microphone and Audio Processing: Used for hands-free voice commands, custom voice shortcuts, and local wake-word activation. Audio is processed locally on the device. Audio recordings are temporary and are never stored or sent to remote servers.
  • Contacts (Read and Write Access): Used to display favorite contacts on the cockpit screen, execute voice-directed calls, dispatch quick SMS notifications, and allow you to view, create, edit, save, and delete contacts directly within the App or an encrypted local vault.
  • Phone State and Quick SMS: Used solely to initiate user-confirmed phone calls and pre-formatted single-tap ETA text messages to recipients you choose. The App does not read your personal incoming text messages.
  • Bluetooth and System State: Used to detect vehicle audio connection and disconnection events, route voice audio, trigger haptic confirmation pulses, and display driving notifications.

2. Cloud Sync and Encrypted Backups

DriveAssist provides an optional multi-device Cloud Sync feature using Google Firebase Firestore:

  • Synced Content: When Cloud Sync is enabled, the App synchronizes user configuration toggles (DataStore preferences, theme selections, audio settings), cockpit layout configurations (Bento tile arrangements), custom voice aliases, saved locations, and address book entries.
  • Client-Side Encryption: All synced records are encrypted on your device using AES-GCM encryption with your user-derived secret key before transmission to Firebase Firestore. We cannot decrypt or view your stored contacts, private aliases, or custom preferences on our servers.

3. Application Analytics and Diagnostics

To monitor cockpit stability, evaluate feature usage, and fix bugs, DriveAssist includes:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Collects anonymous interaction metrics, including screen visits, orientation (portrait vs. landscape), active theme, dynamic color toggles, search method (numeric dialpad vs. text search), map expansion toggles, and external navigation provider selection. These events are linked to randomized Firebase Installation IDs and are never tied to personal contact details or GPS coordinates.
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring: Collects non-identifiable stack traces, operating system versions, and performance latency logs to help us identify and resolve crashes.

4. Machine Learning and Voice Processing

Voice command interpretation and keyword matching in DriveAssist run locally on your device hardware (LiteRT / MediaPipe). Personal user data, voice commands, and contact records are never used, sold, or shared to train machine learning models.

6. External Application Handoffs

When you trigger navigation to an external maps app (such as Google Maps or Waze) or launch external media players, destination coordinates or media playback intents are passed to that application through standard Android Intents. Once handed off, that third-party application's own privacy policy applies.

7. Data Sharing and Selling Prohibition

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, contact records, voice input, or location history. Your data is accessed solely to provide the driving companion features you request.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have full control over your stored data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can purge cloud backups, delete synced database records, or permanently delete your account at any time within the App by navigating to: System Info / Danger Zone / Purge Cloud Data / Delete Account.
  • Off-App Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the App or cannot access your account, you can request permanent deletion of your account and cloud data by emailing support@salinalabs.dev with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process your deletion request within thirty days.

9. Children's Privacy

DriveAssist is intended exclusively for licensed motor vehicle drivers and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
