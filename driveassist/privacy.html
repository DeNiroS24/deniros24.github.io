Privacy Policy for DriveAssist

Effective Date: August 2026

DriveAssist ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Salina Systems, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the DriveAssist mobile application ("App").

Privacy-First Architecture: DriveAssist uses hardware-backed Zero-Knowledge AES-256 encryption. Your private contacts, custom voice aliases, and parking locations are encrypted on your device before any optional cloud backup.

1. Information We Collect & Device Permissions

To provide hands-free driving utilities, DriveAssist requests specific device permissions. Data collected through these permissions remains on your device or is stored in encrypted form:

  • Location Data (GPS & Background Bluetooth): Used to render in-app maps, navigate to saved locations, and automatically save your vehicle's parking spot when Bluetooth disconnects. Location data is never sold or shared.
  • Microphone & Audio: Used for hands-free voice control, custom voice alias matching, and wake-word activation. Audio processing is executed locally on-device whenever possible.
  • Contacts & Phone State: Used to display your VIP contacts, execute voice-activated phone calls, and dispatch quick hands-free SMS messages at your command.
  • System & Device State: Used for Bluetooth connection sensing, vibration feedback, and notification alerts.

2. How We Encrypt & Store Your Data

DriveAssist implements local hardware-backed AES-256 encryption (via SQLCipher and Android KeyStore) for your local DataStore preferences and vault storage. If you enable Cloud Sync, your metadata is encrypted on-device before transmission to our cloud backup service (Firebase Firestore).

3. Third-Party Service Providers

DriveAssist integrates with trusted third-party SDKs strictly to deliver core app services, payment processing, and stability monitoring:

  • RevenueCat & Google Play In-App Billing: Processes subscription orders, free trials, and entitlement verification. Payment credentials (credit card numbers) are handled exclusively by Google Play Store.
  • Google Play Services & Google Maps API: Used to render map tiles, provide geolocation coordinates, and verify app integrity (Play Integrity App Check).
  • Firebase (Google LLC): Used for encrypted cloud backup (Firestore), user authentication, crash reporting (Crashlytics), and app performance monitoring.

4. Data Sharing & Selling Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal data, contacts, voice recordings, or location history. Your information is accessed solely to execute the driving companion features you request.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You maintain total control over your data. You can purge your cloud backups or permanently delete your account at any time directly inside the App under System Info ➔ Danger Zone ➔ Purge Cloud Data / Delete Account.

6. Children's Privacy

DriveAssist is an automotive cockpit companion intended for licensed vehicle drivers and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
