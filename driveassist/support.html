How can we help?

Answers to the most common DriveAssist questions, or reach us directly.

Getting started

Which Android versions are supported?

DriveAssist runs on Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer, on phones, foldables and tablets.

What permissions does DriveAssist need, and why?

Each permission is requested only when you use the related feature: Location for the map, speed and parking; Microphone for voice commands; Contacts and Phone for hands-free calling; SMS for Emergency SOS. You can use the rest of the app if you decline any of them. See the Privacy Policy for the full table.

Do I need an account?

No. DriveAssist works fully as a guest. An account is only needed for Pro encrypted cloud sync.

Voice assistant

Does voice recognition need an internet connection?

No. Speech is recognized on your phone and processed in temporary memory. Nothing is uploaded. If your device is missing its offline speech pack, install it from Android settings (Languages & input → Speech).

The wake word triggers by accident or not at all. What can I do?

Open Control Center → Voice Assistant Identity and adjust Wake Word Sensitivity. Use Low in noisy cabins and High if it misses you. Also check that the microphone permission is granted and that battery optimization isn't restricting the app.

Will DriveAssist pause my music?

No. Standby listening never ducks or pauses audio. Audio focus is requested only while you are actively speaking a command.

Emergency SOS

How do I set up Emergency SOS?
  1. Open Control Center → Interaction & Driving Safety.
  2. Tap Configure SOS.
  3. Pick your emergency contacts and, on dual-SIM phones, the SIM to send from.
  4. Grant the SMS and Phone permissions when asked.

The status badge changes from Not Set to Configured when you're ready.

What happens when I trigger SOS?

DriveAssist texts your emergency contacts a message with a live GPS map link. If you enabled SOS Triggers Phone Call Also, it also calls your primary contact. Standard carrier SMS rates may apply, and SOS needs cellular service. It does not replace calling local emergency services.

Navigation & parking

Which navigation apps can I use?

Google Maps and Waze. Choose one under Control Center → Navigation Engine. If the app you pick isn't installed, DriveAssist offers a one-tap link to install it.

How does automatic parking detection work?

When your phone disconnects from your car's Bluetooth audio, DriveAssist saves your location. Tap the Parking card to get walking directions back, or long-press it to clear the spot. You can also mark a spot manually at any time.

Vault & security

I forgot my master password. Can you reset it?

No, and that is by design. Your vault is zero-knowledge, so Salina Labs cannot read or reset it. Use your 12-word recovery phrase on the recovery screen to regain access. Store that phrase somewhere safe and offline.

Where do I find my recovery phrase?

Open Security Vault → View 12-Word Recovery Phrase and confirm with biometrics or your master password.

I deleted a contact or shortcut by mistake.

Deleted items stay recoverable for 90 days. Open Contacts → Recently Deleted, select the items and tap Restore.

Pro subscription

What does Pro include and how much is it?

Pro adds unlimited wake words, a customizable Bento grid, encrypted cloud sync and more. It's $4.99 per month or $34.99 per year, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

Open Control Center → Pro Subscription and tap Manage Subscription on Google Play to cancel. On a new device, tap Restore Purchases.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Items beyond Free limits are kept encrypted on your device and marked Pro Locked. They re-activate instantly if you resubscribe.

Account & data

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, open Driver Hub → Permanently Delete Account and re-authenticate. This removes all cloud records and your sign-in. Cancel any active Google Play subscription separately in Google Play. To only remove cloud backups, use Purge Cloud Backups. To request deletion without the app, email support@salinalabs.dev from your account email.
