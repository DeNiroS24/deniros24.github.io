Features

Everything within reach. Nothing to distract you.

Built around glanceable design, big touch targets and voice-first control.

Cockpit

A dashboard that fits the way you drive

Arrange speed, weather, navigation, media and trip cards into your own Bento grid, in light or dark, with frosted-glass styling.

  • Live 3D map with speed-adaptive camera and tap-to-route points of interest
  • Adapts to phones, tablets and landscape layouts
  • Eco mode throttles GPS and rendering when you are stationary
Voice

Just say it. Even with no signal.

Call, navigate or send an SOS with your voice. Recognition runs entirely on your phone, so your speech never leaves the car.

  • Custom wake word and your own spoken shortcuts
  • Never ducks or pauses your music while listening
  • Pauses automatically during phone calls
Calling & SOS

The people who matter, one tap away

Pin VIP contacts for instant speed dial. If something goes wrong, DriveAssist can text your live location to your emergency contacts.

  • Emergency SMS with a live GPS link, with dual-SIM selection
  • Optional simultaneous call to your primary contact
  • Confirmation guards prevent accidental calls while driving
Parking

Never forget where you parked

DriveAssist marks your spot automatically when your car's Bluetooth disconnects, then walks you back with one tap.

  • Distance, direction and street address at a glance
  • One-tap walking directions in your preferred navigation app
  • Works with Google Maps or Waze
Telemetry

Know your drive in real time

A high-contrast HUD with trip computers, compass, G-force meter and tire-pressure indicators, tuned for daytime and night driving.

  • Dual-slot trip odometer with Kalman-filtered distance
  • Tire-pressure colors that escalate from normal to critical
  • One-tap MPH and KM/H switching
Security

A vault only you can open

Contacts, shortcuts and destinations are encrypted on your device. Your master password and 12-word recovery phrase never reach our servers.

  • Hardware-backed AES-256-GCM via Android Keystore
  • Biometric unlock and configurable auto-lock
  • Deleted items kept 90 days so mistakes are recoverable
Privacy by design

Built so we can't see your data

Automotive software shouldn't track drivers. DriveAssist is engineered around that belief, not just a policy page.

Zero-knowledge vault

Keys are generated in your device's secure hardware. Support staff cannot decrypt your vault or recover a lost password.

Speech stays on-device

Voice recognition runs locally in volatile memory. No recordings are uploaded and nothing is streamed to the cloud.

No advertising IDs

No ad SDKs, no behavioral profiling and no data brokers. Ad-ID permission is stripped at the manifest level.

You stay in control

GDPR and CCPA/CPRA rights supported, with in-app cloud backup purge and full account deletion.
